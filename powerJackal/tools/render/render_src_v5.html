Board

Outline
72.4 × 47.0 mm, 1.5 mm corner radius. 12% less area than the earlier 76.2 × 50.8 mm layout, with the same hole pattern.
Holes
4 × 4.5 mm close fit for #8-32 VEX screws, 63.5 × 38.1 mm apart (5 × 3 VEX pitches), 4.45 mm from the edges. Copper stays 4.2 mm clear of each hole.
Stackup
2 layers, 1.6 mm, 1 oz. Top carries the parts and most routing. Bottom is almost one solid ground plane.
Finish
Black mask, white silk, ENIG pads (shown). Any colour works.
Parts
59 placed parts, all on the top side, plus 9 bare copper test pads.
Tallest
J1 jack 11.5 mm, C1 11.2 mm, J2 terminal 10 mm

Zones

    How it is laid out

    • Inputs on the left edge (J1 smart port, J2 bench), outputs on the right edge (J6 5 V, J9 3.3 V), USB-C and I2C on the bottom edge.
    • Each INA226 sits beside its shunt. The two sense lines run in the gap between the shunt pads and enter each pad from its inner edge. No load current flows in them.
    • The TPSM53602 sits between its input caps (left) and output caps (right), each side in its own copper pour. The feedback divider sits right at the FB pin.
    • The ESP32-C3 module faces its USB pins toward the USB-C port. D+ and D− are fixed copper from the connector through the ESD chip and the 22 Ω resistors to the module.
    • Eleven thermal vias in the TPSM53602 exposed pad and about 200 stitching vias tie the top ground pour to the bottom plane.
    • J1 still uses a stand-in footprint. Check it against the Molex 95501 drawing before ordering boards.
